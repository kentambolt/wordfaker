Party game for one phone

Word Faker

Everyone gets the same secret word. One of you is faking it. Find out who.

    How to play

    Pass the phone around. When your name is on the screen, swipe up to see your word. One of you sees WORD FAKER instead. Once everyone has looked, the app picks who starts. Take turns talking about the word without saying it. The Word Faker has to bluff, and the rest of you have to catch them. At the end, tap to reveal.

    Word Faker is a free party word game for one phone. No app, no sign-up. Play in English, Danish, Spanish, French, German, Ukrainian and Russian.
